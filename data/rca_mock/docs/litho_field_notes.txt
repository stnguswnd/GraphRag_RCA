Lithography Field Notes
Process Step: LITHO

1. Edge-Loc defects from focus drift

The Edge-Loc pattern shows failing die localized to one or more sectors of the wafer
edge, rather than a continuous ring. In lithography, a recurring cause is focus drift
near the wafer edge. When the exposure focus drifts away from best focus at the
periphery, the aerial image blurs and the pattern fails locally, yielding localized
edge defects. This should be read as distinct from an Edge-Ring signature, which is
continuous and omni-directional around the wafer.

Focus drift is a process-parameter deviation (Parameter type cause) and is tied to
the focus parameter departing from its target — often reading low relative to best
focus at the edge field.

2. Confirming the cause

Focus telemetry for the exposure tool should be reviewed across the suspect window.
Because focus drift is a parameter deviation rather than a hardware failure, the most
useful checks are telemetry and the negative-control normal-lot ratio, together with
locating the yield change point to bound when the drift began.
