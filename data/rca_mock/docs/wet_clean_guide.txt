Wet Clean Guide — Mechanical Damage
Process Step: CLEAN

1. Scratch defects from megasonic overpower

During wet cleaning, megasonic energy is used to dislodge particles from the wafer
surface. If the megasonic power is set too high, the acoustic cavitation becomes
aggressive enough to damage fragile structures. Excessive megasonic power can induce
micro-scratches, which are occasionally seen at electrical test as a Scratch pattern.

Megasonic overpower is a recipe/method problem (Method type cause) rather than a
worn part, and it is tied to the megasonic power parameter running high. Note that a
Scratch signature is ambiguous: it can originate here in CLEAN (megasonic overpower)
or upstream in CMP (slurry particle contamination), so the two candidate causes must
be weighed against the lot route and telemetry.

2. Confirming the cause

Megasonic power telemetry for the clean tool should be checked first. Because this is
a method/recipe issue, alarm history around the window is relevant, the normal-lot
ratio provides a negative control, and the yield change point bounds the onset.
